# Third-Party Licensing and Output Ownership

## Third-party components
Open fonts and third-party software incorporated, downloaded, or used by PSE remain governed by their own license terms. PSE does not relicense them or reduce rights granted by those upstream licenses.

Before public beta, release packaging should enumerate bundled third-party components and their notices where required.

## Generated projects
Users may modify generated PSE boilerplate/configuration and use it in their projects under the permissions ultimately granted by the PSE license. Generated boilerplate does not grant PSE ownership over user content.

## User content and output
PSE makes no ownership claim merely by processing or typesetting:

- manuscripts;
- translations;
- illustrations or photographs;
- datasets;
- user metadata;
- final PDF/publication content.

Community publication attribution is a software-license compliance condition, not an ownership notice over the book.
