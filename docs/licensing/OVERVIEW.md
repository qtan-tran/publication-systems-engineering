# Licensing overview

Publication Systems Engineering is currently **source-available in development**. The final public licence has not yet been published.

The intended operating model distinguishes between:

- qualifying community/individual use;
- institutional and commercial publishing use;
- enterprise, embedding, OEM, hosted, or managed-service use.

The exact legal instruments for these categories remain subject to independent legal review. Until the final licence is published, source visibility does not itself grant commercial, enterprise, embedding, OEM, white-label, or managed-service rights.

## Attribution

PSE currently treats publication attribution as an **operational release contract**: every ebook produced by `pse release` must contain the bundled canonical attribution banner on a copyright/publication page or colophon. The standard publication page inserts it automatically, and the release pipeline records render evidence in the release manifest.

The banner includes the canonical English line:

> **Produced with Publication Systems Engineering.**

The public brand specification is documented in [Brand and attribution](../design/BRAND-AND-ATTRIBUTION.md).

The final licence may separately make attribution a legal condition. The wording, scope, enforceability, exceptions, and remedies of any such licence obligation remain subject to independent legal review; the release pipeline does not itself constitute legal approval.

## Output ownership

Using PSE does not transfer ownership of a user's manuscript, translation, images, metadata, or publication content to the PSE project. Third-party fonts and dependencies remain governed by their own licences.
