# Commercial Licensing Inquiry Workflow

Status: **staging workflow; commercial licensing is not yet open until a public contact channel and legally reviewed terms are published.**

PSE reserves commercial use for a separate commercial license unless final published terms explicitly say otherwise.

## Who should inquire

A commercial inquiry is appropriate for organizations or paid production workflows that intend to deploy PSE as part of commercial publishing infrastructure and are not clearly covered by the final community license.

Do not assume that repository visibility, ability to fork, or technical installation grants commercial-use rights.

## Information an inquiry should contain

Only business-level information is needed initially:

- organization name;
- country/jurisdiction;
- organization type;
- intended deployment scope;
- approximate number of users/teams;
- whether PSE will be used internally, for clients, or as part of a hosted service;
- approximate publication volume where relevant;
- desired start date;
- contact person.

## Do not send

Do **not** attach:

- unpublished manuscripts;
- author contracts;
- credentials/secrets;
- recipient proof files;
- proprietary production data.

## Public contact gate

Before a public licensed beta, the repository maintainer must publish a concrete commercial-licensing contact channel. Until that exists, the correct status is **commercial licensing inquiries not yet open**, not an invented placeholder email address.

Pricing, royalties, exemptions, and enterprise terms belong in commercial agreements, not in the software community-license text.
