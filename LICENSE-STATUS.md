# License Status

Publication Systems Engineering is currently **source-available in development** and is **not yet released under a final public licence**.

The repository intentionally does not contain an operative root `LICENSE` until independent legal review is complete.

Current release gates:

1. independent review of the final community/commercial licensing instruments;
2. live CI on the exact release candidate across supported operating systems;
3. authoritative Git-history and secret scanning for the public candidate history.

Do not describe the project as OSI open source unless and until its final licence qualifies.
