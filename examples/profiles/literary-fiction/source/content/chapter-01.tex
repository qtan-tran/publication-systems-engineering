\chapter{The Quiet Archive}
At seven in the morning the reading room was still empty. Mara placed the small grey box on the table and waited for the dust to settle.

Nothing on the label explained why the box had been kept. There was only a date, a shelf mark, and a name she did not recognise. She opened the lid.

\bigskip
Inside lay three sheets of paper, folded once. The first began with a sentence that seemed less like a record than an invitation: \emph{If you have found this, begin with the room.}

Mara looked up. The room had not changed, yet the instruction made every ordinary thing—the lamps, the long table, the locked cabinet at the wall—appear newly deliberate.

\section*{Later}
By noon, she had read the three sheets twice. Their meaning was no clearer, but the silence around them had acquired a shape.
