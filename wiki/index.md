# Publication Systems Engineering

Publication Systems Engineering (PSE) is a reproducible book-production framework that separates manuscript meaning, publication-type defaults, and page presentation.

This documentation is written first for authors, editors, translators, and other book practitioners. You do not need to understand TeX or the internal architecture to begin.
